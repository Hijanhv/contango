# Naming — why "Contango"

**Contango** = the market term for a forward curve trading *above* spot (an upward-sloping term structure). Naming the protocol after the exact structure it builds — an on-chain FX **forward curve** — is a deliberate credibility signal.

## Why it fits (for judges who know derivatives)
- **Instant competence signal.** The judges are Circle ecosystem + DevRel + finance-savvy people. "Contango" tells them *"these founders understand FX/rates markets"* before the pitch even starts — the credibility that carries a quant DeFi entry.
- **Ownable + memorable.** No crypto-brand collision; single distinctive word; great for a logo/wordmark.
- **Ties to the flagship flourish:** "Arc's first on-chain stablecoin FX forward curve — the primitive capital markets are built on."

## The one nuance to prep for (turn a gotcha into a credibility moment)
A curve is "in contango" when **forwards > spot**. For **EURUSD specifically**, with USD rates above EUR rates, EURUSD forwards trade at a slight *discount* — that pair leans **backwardation**, not contango.

A sharp judge may notice. Answer confidently:
> *"Contango names the **category** — we're building the forward **curve** itself, quoting many pairs and tenors; plenty are in contango (e.g., USD vs lower-rate/EM currencies). Direction depends on the pair; the brand is the structure, not a claim about one cross."*

That reply demonstrates you understand the mechanics precisely — which is the point.

## Alternatives considered (rejected)
- **Outright** — the literal FX term for a forward; maximum precision but a plain English word (weaker brand). *(was the working name)*
- **Basis** — the spread/carry captured; clean but echoes the defunct 2018 "Basis" stablecoin.
- **Carry** — approachable, names the yield; too generic.
